01 / ABOUT

I Build.
I Create.
I Tell Stories.

I am an Information Technology student drawn to both the logic of systems and the beauty of creation.

I build with code, create through design, capture through photography, and tell stories through the things I make. My work exists somewhere between the technical and the artistic—where function meets form, and where every detail has a purpose.

I believe good work should not only work well, but feel intentional. Clean code. Thoughtful design. Honest stories.

I am still learning, still creating, and still finding new ways to turn ideas into something real.
